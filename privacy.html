Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 15, 2026

1. Data Controller

NomadX AI operates the NomadX mobile application and this website. Questions and privacy requests can be sent to support@nomadxai.online.

2. Processed Personal Data

Depending on the features you choose to use, we process:

  • Account data: email address, username, and optional profile name and biography.
  • Social sign-in data: if you choose Sign in with Google or Apple, the identity provider sends NomadX the account identifiers needed to sign you in, such as name, email address, and user ID. Its SDK may also process a phone number if supplied by the account, device or usage identifiers, IP-derived coarse location, and other security data according to that provider's privacy disclosures. NomadX does not use this data for advertising or tracking.
  • Profile and social data: username, optional name and biography, current country, follows, message requests, room messages, and private messages. Uploading public profile media is disabled in the current release. Profile media uploaded in an earlier test version is kept private and unavailable to app users while it is reviewed or deleted.
  • Community safety data: Community Terms acceptance, reports you submit, a limited snapshot of reported content, moderation status, moderator notes, and related audit records.
  • Optional location data: your device location is requested only when you choose to verify and update your current country. NomadX converts that location to a country and stores the country, country code, and a verification indicator on your profile; it does not store the precise coordinates for this action. AI Daily Route is marked Coming Soon and cannot be used in the current store release, so that feature does not collect or send new route requests.
  • Data kept on your device: visited-country history, expenses, visa entries, packing lists, travel documents, bookmarks, and most preferences are stored locally unless a feature clearly states otherwise.
  • Technical data: service providers may receive IP address, device/browser information, and request logs needed to deliver and protect their services.
  • Optional product analytics: if you enable Share Product Analytics in Settings, NomadX records an allowlisted feature-event name, event time, platform, app version, and limited non-sensitive configuration such as interface language or transport preference. Analytics does not include messages, notes, email in event properties, route coordinates, or live location.

3. Purposes of Data Processing

We use data to authenticate accounts, provide profiles and messaging, synchronize the features you request, enforce Community Terms, filter and review community content, investigate reports, protect the service, answer support requests, and comply with law. AI Daily Route and paid Pro features remain unavailable unless the app clearly presents them as active in a future release.

Optional product analytics is used to understand feature reliability and adoption. It remains disabled for your account until you opt in from Settings and does not control access to core NomadX features.

4. Service Providers and External Links

Cloud account, database, storage, and server functions are provided by Supabase; the NomadX project is hosted in the Frankfurt region. Google Identity Services and Sign in with Apple are used only when you choose the corresponding sign-in option. Map tiles, weather information, and exchange rates are requested from third-party services such as CARTO/OpenStreetMap, Open-Meteo, and ExchangeRate-API. Their own privacy terms apply to those requests.

AI Daily Route integrations are disabled in the current store release. The current release does not send starting coordinates, route preferences, or place candidates to an AI or place provider. This policy will be updated before that feature becomes available.

The current store release does not include affiliate booking or travel-provider purchase flows. If such integrations are introduced later, this policy and the in-app disclosure will be updated before they are made available.

5. Retention and Security

Account data is retained while your account is active and as required for security or legal obligations. You can permanently delete your account from Settings in the mobile app. The deletion flow removes your authentication account, profile, social data, messages, and profile media, and clears NomadX local data on that device. Local data can also be removed by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage.

Reports, moderation decisions, and audit records may be retained for service safety, abuse prevention, dispute handling, and legal compliance even after the related content is removed, to the extent permitted or required by law.

Optional product-event history is intended to be retained for no more than 90 days. Turning Share Product Analytics off deletes your event history and activity summary immediately.

We use access controls, row-level database security, encrypted network connections, and restricted server credentials. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Choices and Rights

You may access and edit profile data in the app, choose whether to grant location or photo access, and delete your account in Settings. You can enable or withdraw optional product analytics at any time from the same screen. Depending on your location, you may also have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing and to receive a copy of your data. Contact support@nomadxai.online or use the web account deletion page.

7. Children and Changes

NomadX is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so it can be reviewed and removed. We will update the date above when this policy changes materially.
