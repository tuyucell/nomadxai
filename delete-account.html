Delete Your NomadX Account

Last Updated: July 26, 2026

Delete directly in the app

Open NomadX AI and go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account. Follow the confirmation steps to permanently delete your account and associated server data.

Request deletion without the app

If you cannot access the app, email support@nomadxai.online from the email address registered to your NomadX account. Use the subject NomadX account deletion request. We may ask you to verify account ownership before completing the request.

What is deleted

Deletion covers your authentication account, profile, follows, message requests, messages, reports, profile media, and optional product analytics associated with the account. Data stored only on your device can be removed by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.

Limited records may be retained only when required for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legal compliance. See the Privacy Policy for details.
