NomadX Support

For account access, privacy, safety reports, or technical problems, email support@nomadxai.online.

Before contacting us

  • Include your app version, device model, and a short description of the problem.
  • Do not send passwords, verification codes, payment details, or precise live location.
  • For abusive content, use the in-app Report and Block actions first, then contact support if needed.

Account deletion

You can permanently delete your account from Profile → Settings → Delete Account. See the account deletion instructions for details.

Response handling

Safety, abuse, privacy, and account-access reports are prioritized. Response time can vary with the complexity of the request.
